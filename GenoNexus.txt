GenoNexus Platform: Comprehensive
Technical Implementation Guide
Executive Summary
This report provides a detailed analysis of the GenoNexus platform, which is built upon a revolutionary Three-Portal Architecture connecting Researchers, Clinicians, and Patients into a unified ecosystem. It evaluates the 15 core GenoNexus features, including their
documented success rates from recent academic research (2024-2025), latest peer-reviewed
references, and recommended backend technologies and frameworks for implementation. The
analysis is based on extensive research from Google Scholar, peer-reviewed journals, and industry
reports published between 2024 and 2025.

The Three-Portal Architecture:
1. Researcher Portal: A powerful command center for scientists featuring AI-driven DNA analysis and global collaboration.
2. Clinical Portal: A streamlined interface for doctors to ingest patient genomic files and coordinate specialist referrals.
3. Patient Portal: A secure dashboard empowering everyday users to understand their personal genetic health insights.

Part 3: Implementation Architecture

Three-Portal Ecosystem (Next.js App Router)
The frontend of GenoNexus is divided into three distinct portals to seamlessly connect all stakeholders:
1. Researcher Portal (/dashboard): Command center for advanced AI and genomics analysis.
2. Clinical Portal (/dashboard + clinical scopes): Ingestion pipelines and specialist referrals.
3. Patient Portal (/user): Intuitive dashboards for personal health tracking.

Microservices Architecture
The GenoNexus platform is recommended to be implemented using a microservices architecture
with the following core services:
1. Genomic Analysis Service (FastAPI)
Multi-format DNA analysis
Variant pathogenicity prediction
CRISPR target design
ncRNA function prediction
2. AI/ML Service (FastAPI + PyTorch)
Gene chatbot backend
Drug-gene interaction prediction
Disease outbreak prediction
Longitudinal health trajectory modeling
3. Visualization Service (Node.js + Express)
3D genome browser

Digital cell twin simulation
Phylogenetic tree generation
Virus mutation tracking
4. Collaboration Service (Node.js + Express)
Research hub management
Pipeline builder backend
Real-time collaboration features
5. Security & Privacy Service (FastAPI)
Blockchain data sovereignty
Zero-knowledge proof generation
User authentication and authorization
